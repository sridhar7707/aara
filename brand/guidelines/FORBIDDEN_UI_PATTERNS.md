# Forbidden UI Patterns

Sentinel Intelligence is an auditable decision governance platform—never a retail trading app or a speculative prediction tool.

## 1. Prohibited Trading UI Patterns
* ❌ **Flashing Live Tick Tickers:** No green/red blinking price updates.
* ❌ **Candlestick Charts:** No retail red/green stock or crypto candles.
* ❌ **Urgency Action Triggers:** No "BUY NOW", "QUICK TRADE", or FOMO call-to-action buttons.
* ❌ **Market Direction Arrows:** No speculative prediction arrows indicating future price targets.
* ❌ **Gimmick Gauges:** No "AI Confidence Meters", radial probability arcs, or "Fear & Greed" dials.

## 2. Prohibited AI Marketing Copy
* ❌ *"AI Predicts..."*
* ❌ *"AI Beats the Market..."*
* ❌ *"Guaranteed Returns..."*
* ❌ *"Smart Automated Bot..."*

## 3. Approved Executive Counter-Patterns
* ✅ Quiet tabular metrics with `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`.
* ✅ Explicit policy Pass/Fail verification checklists.
* ✅ Immutable cryptographic audit fingerprint references.
