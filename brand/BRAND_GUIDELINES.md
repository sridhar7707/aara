## Brand Voice & Tone

### Voice (Personality)

**Sentinel Intelligence speaks like:**
- A trusted advisor (not a salesbot)
- An institutional expert (not a retail influencer)
- Someone who explains clearly (not jargon-heavy)
- A partner in governance (not a replacement for judgment)

**Word Choices:**
```
✅ decision         ❌ prediction
✅ conviction       ❌ confidence (for ML)
✅ evidence         ❌ signals
✅ governance       ❌ control
✅ auditable        ❌ trackable
✅ process quality  ❌ performance
✅ clarity          ❌ magic
```

### Tone (Emotional Register)

**Professional but warm:**
- Clear and straightforward (no jargon)
- Confident but humble (we help, don't guarantee)
- Respectful of investor expertise (not condescending)
- Serious about governance (never flippant)

**Examples:**

❌ **Wrong:**
> "Our AI never misses. Guaranteed returns with zero risk."

✅ **Right:**
> "Decision Conviction: 82/100. Evidence supports this thesis. Governance rules passed. Your approval moves forward."

❌ **Wrong:**
> "We predict the market with 87% accuracy."

✅ **Right:**
> "This decision aligns with your governance rules and supported by available evidence. You decide."

## Messaging Framework

### Hero Message (What We Are)

**For decision-makers:**
> "Sentinel Intelligence is the governed decision cockpit for serious investors. AI proposes. You decide. Every decision is auditable."

**For compliance/risk officers:**
> "Institutional-grade governance for AI-assisted decision making. Rules enforced. Every action logged. Capital preserved."

### Key Messages (What We Do)

1. **Decision Governance**
   - "We structure your decision process, not replace it."
   - "Governance rules are checkpoints, not suggestions."

2. **Evidence-First**
   - "We show you the evidence, not the crystal ball."
   - "Conviction scores reflect process quality, not market predictions."

3. **Complete Auditability**
   - "Every decision is traceable to governance rules and evidence."
   - "Chain of Custody shows who decided what, and why."

4. **Risk Management First**
   - "Capital preservation is not optional."
   - "Risk Governor ensures safety guardrails stay in place."

### What We're NOT (Refusals)

Never say Sentinel Intelligence is:
- ❌ A stock picker or market predictor
- ❌ A "set it and forget it" automation
- ❌ An alternative to your judgment
- ❌ A get-rich-quick tool
- ❌ Powered by "magic" or "black box" AI
- ❌ Guaranteed to beat the market

## Usage Guidelines

### ✅ DO

**In Marketing & Communications:**
- ✅ Emphasize governance and transparency
- ✅ Show the decision process, not the outcome
- ✅ Use investor testimonials about process confidence
- ✅ Highlight audit trail and Chain of Custody
- ✅ Explain evidence quality, not AI certainty
- ✅ Reference your governance framework

**In Product UI:**
- ✅ Display metrics as integers (e.g., "Conviction: 82/100")
- ✅ Show governance status clearly
- ✅ Use emerald for "approved," gold for "caution"
- ✅ Explain evidence provenance
- ✅ Make decision process transparent
- ✅ Show what's auditable, what's logged

**In Documentation:**
- ✅ Reference governance rules
- ✅ Explain evidence sources
- ✅ Use Chain of Custody language
- ✅ Mention institutional-grade standards
- ✅ Show decision quality metrics
- ✅ Reference compliance frameworks

### ❌ DON'T

**Messaging to Avoid:**
- ❌ "AI that never fails"
- ❌ "Guaranteed returns"
- ❌ "Beat the market"
- ❌ "Set it and forget it"
- ❌ "Our algorithm knows better than you"
- ❌ "Predict the future"
- ❌ Hype or marketing-speak

**Visual Choices to Avoid:**
- ❌ Neon colors or bright gradients
- ❌ Red for warnings (use navy or emerald instead)
- ❌ Dark mode backgrounds
- ❌ Percentage signs after confidence scores (use "82/100" not "82%")
- ❌ Distorting or modifying the logo
- ❌ Using colors outside the frozen palette

**Product Terminology to Avoid:**
- ❌ "AI Confidence" (use "Model Agreement" or "Conviction")
- ❌ "Recommendation" (use "Decision")
- ❌ "Prediction" (use "Evidence")
- ❌ "Trust Score" (use "Governance Evaluation")
- ❌ "Trading Bot" (we're a Decision Intelligence Platform)
